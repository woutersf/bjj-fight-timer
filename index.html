Fight Timer is a free online interval timer for BJJ (Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu), Judo, boxing, MMA, wrestling and grappling. Set your round length and rest time, pick a bell sound, and hit Start — the screen stays on automatically. Works as an installable PWA on iOS and Android. No account, no subscription, free forever.
